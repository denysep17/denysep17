## `> who am i`

I work on **AI control, deceptive behavior evaluation, agent oversight, and technical mechanisms for verifiable AI systems**.

My research direction connects two questions:

> **How do we understand and control what increasingly capable AI systems do?**

and

> **How do we verify where, how, and under what constraints those systems execute?**

## `? research questions`

```text
01 → Can models recognize when they are being evaluated?

02 → How robust are scheming signals under trigger,
     context, and oversight perturbations?

03 → What control protocols remain useful as agents
     become increasingly capable and autonomous?

04 → How can behavioral evidence and infrastructure
     evidence reinforce each other?

05 → Can we verify where, when, and under what
     constraints model inference occurred?

06 → Can hardware-rooted evidence improve the
     credibility of AI deployment claims?

07 → What should an end-to-end verification layer
     for advanced AI systems look like?
```
